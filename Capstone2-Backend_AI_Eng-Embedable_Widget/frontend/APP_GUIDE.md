# LeadForge � AI Widget & Lead Capture Platform

## Purpose

LeadForge lets businesses embed an AI-powered chat widget on any website to engage visitors, qualify them as leads, and capture their information � all automatically, without manual follow-up.

---

## Problem It Solves

Most website visitors leave without taking action. Businesses lose potential customers because:

- Static contact forms have low conversion rates
- Sales teams can't engage every visitor in real-time
- There's no way to know which visitors are genuinely interested

Leadforge replaces passive forms with an intelligent AI assistant that talks to visitors, identifies intent, and delivers only qualified leads to the business.

---

## Using the App � Step by Step

### 1. Create an Account
Register at `/register` and log in. A personal organization is created for you automatically.

### 2. Set Up an Organization & Project
Go to **Organizations** ? create or select one ? then create a **Project** inside it. The project holds your widget configuration and API key.

### 3. Configure the Widget
Inside your project, set the AI instructions � what the widget should ask, how it should respond, and what qualifies a lead for your business.

### 4. Embed the Widget
Copy the single `<script>` snippet from the **Widget** page and paste it into your website's HTML. No other integration is needed.

### 5. Monitor Leads & Conversations
From the **Dashboard**, track:
- Total leads captured
- Qualified vs. unqualified leads
- Conversion rate
- Full conversation history per lead
