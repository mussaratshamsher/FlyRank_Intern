# Before & After Editing

This assignment required editing AI-generated writing so it reflects my own voice instead of sounding generic.

---

## Before (Generic AI)

> Results-driven software engineer passionate about innovation and cutting-edge technologies. Experienced in delivering high-quality solutions using modern technologies.

### Why I Changed It

This sounds generic and could describe almost any developer. It doesn't explain what I actually build or what makes my work different.

---

## After (My Version)

> I enjoy building AI-powered applications that solve practical problems. My focus is on creating tools that save time, improve productivity, and provide a better user experience through modern AI technologies.

### Why It's Better

- It reflects my actual work.
- It explains the kind of problems I solve.
- It sounds like something I would naturally say.
- It avoids unnecessary buzzwords.
